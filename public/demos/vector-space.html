minimem Vector Space

What a vector looks like

Every fact minimem stores becomes 384 numbers. These are real vectors from the all-minilm model for 21 example facts and one query. The 3D view is a flattened shadow of the real 384-dimensional space.

1. The facts in 3D

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover a point to see its three truly nearest neighbors. The amber diamond is the query “suggest something for dinner”.

2. Who is actually nearest in 384 dimensions

The picture can mislead, so this list uses the true cosine similarity in all 384 dimensions. It is exactly what Qdrant computes for the semantic signal.

    3. One vector, all 384 numbers

    Each column is one dimension. Amber is positive, teal is negative, brighter means larger. No single column means anything you could name. Only the whole pattern, compared with another pattern, carries meaning.

    4. Every fact against every other

    Each cell is the cosine similarity of two facts, with a topic color strip on each edge. Darker is more similar. Facts of one topic form faint blocks, but the contrast is modest, which is typical of a small embedding model.
